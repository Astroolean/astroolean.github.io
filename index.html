Overview

Custom Bots is a single-file GSC mod for Call of Duty: Black Ops 2 Zombies on the Plutonium T6 client. It spawns fully autonomous AI bots that play like real human teammates.

These bots don't follow you around like bodyguards. They make their own decisions. They roam the map independently, train zombies into hordes, open doors, buy perks and weapons, pick up buildable parts, revive downed teammates, save the last zombie at the end of every round, knife in early rounds to maximize points, and fight their way through high rounds entirely on their own.

Every bot runs an autonomous decision engine that evaluates all available actions each tick and picks the smartest one based on current context: ammo, health, points, round number, nearby threats, and personality. No two bots make the same choices. An aggressive bot pushes for weapons while a cautious bot prioritizes perks and safety.

The entire system is contained in a single file: custom_bots.gsc. No compilation. No dependencies. No external tools. Drop it in and play.

Installation

Step 1 — Locate Your Plutonium T6 Folder

Open your Plutonium launcher and find your T6 installation directory. The default location is:

%LOCALAPPDATA%\Plutonium\storage\t6\

Step 2 — Navigate to the Scripts Folder

Inside the T6 folder, go to (or create if it doesn't exist):

storage/t6/scripts/zm/

Step 3 — Drop the File

Copy custom_bots.gsc into the zm folder. Your final path should be:

storage/t6/scripts/zm/custom_bots.gsc

Step 4 — Launch and Play

Start Plutonium and begin a Custom Games Zombies match on any map. The bots spawn automatically at the start of round 1.

Updating? If you replace custom_bots.gsc with a newer version, you must fully restart Plutonium (close and reopen the launcher). Restarting only the match causes a "Client/Server game mismatch" error because GSC scripts are loaded at client startup.

Quick Start

No configuration needed. By default, the mod spawns 3 bots at Shotguns rank (the highest skill tier). Just install the file and start playing.

Want to customize? Open the in-game console (~ tilde key) and type any of the commands in the Configuration Reference below.

Autonomous Decision Engine

The brain of every bot. Instead of a rigid priority chain, each bot evaluates all available actions every tick and picks the one with the highest utility score. This makes bots genuinely think for themselves.

How It Works

Every 100ms, bot_evaluate_best_action() calculates a score for every possible action, then the bot executes whichever scored highest. Scoring factors include:

  • Ammo level — 15% ammo scores "refill" much higher than 80% ammo
  • Health — 30% health scores "flee" much higher
  • Points — 5000+ points boosts PaP score
  • Round number — Higher rounds boost box/PaP, reduce wallbuy scores
  • Nearby threats — More zombies boost flee and training scores
  • Personality — Aggressive bots push weapons, cautious bots push perks

Why This Matters

In a traditional priority system, a bot with low ammo, no Juggernog, and 3000 points would always buy Jug because "perks > ammo." But a smart player refills ammo first — you can't survive the walk to the perk machine with 500 rounds. The decision engine handles this correctly. Each bot evaluates its own unique situation every single tick.

Advanced Combat Engine

The combat engine is the core of the bot AI. It runs every frame and handles target acquisition, aiming, firing, grenades, fleeing, and strafing. Every component is rank-scaled.

Processing Pipeline

  1. Knife Mode Check — If early-round knife mode is active, all shooting is suppressed.
  2. Validate State — Confirm the bot is alive, not climbing, not in a menu.
  3. Crowd Census — Count nearby zombies within detection range.
  4. Acquire Target — Weighted scoring with triple LOS validation.
  5. Validate Target — Confirm target is alive, visible, not stale (3-frame clearing).
  6. Flee Decision — Multi-factor analysis: health, crowd, damage, proximity, training state.
  7. Grenade Check — Evaluate whether to throw at a dense cluster.
  8. Combat Movement — Strafe perpendicular while maintaining kite distance.
  9. Aim & Fire — Full pipeline with triple LOS and zero-fallback shooting.

Triple Line-of-Sight Verification

Every shot must pass three independent LOS checks:

  1. botsighttracepassed(ent) — Engine's built-in bot AI sight check.
  2. SightTracePassed(eye, centroid) — Ray trace to the zombie's chest height.
  3. SightTracePassed(eye, feet+10) — Ray trace to the zombie's feet.

All three must pass before a single bullet is fired. No fallback. No guessing. If the aim point fails LOS, the bot stops shooting entirely.

Burst Fire & Ammo Conservation

  • Burst Size — Bones fires 5–20 shot bursts (panic spray). Shotguns fires 3–5 (disciplined).
  • Burst Pause — Bones pauses 200–600ms between bursts. Shotguns pauses 30–80ms.
  • Ammo Conservation — Below 40% ammo, the ammo_conserve stat activates probabilistic shot suppression. Below 30%, bursts cap at 3 shots and pauses increase 50%.
  • Range-Gated Firing — Max engagement range: 600 units normal, 400 at 50% ammo, 250 at 25%. Bots won't waste ammo on distant zombies unless surrounded.

Reaction Time & Jitter

New target acquisition has a rank-scaled delay: Bones at 800ms, Shotguns at 50ms. Each acquisition adds random ±50ms jitter to prevent robotic consistency. A separate target_switch_delay prevents jittery hopping (Bones: 1200ms, Shotguns: 100ms).

Focus Accuracy

Bots improve aim the longer they track the same target. After 1 second: scatter reduced 15%. After 2 seconds: reduced 25%. Resets on target switch.

Grenade Intelligence

  • Bones requires 7+ zombies bunched together. Shotguns throws at 5.
  • Rank-scaled cooldowns: Bones = 20s, Shotguns = 12s.
  • Ammo-gated: won't throw when gun ammo is below 25%.
  • Range band: 300–800 units only. No grenades in rounds 1–3 unless 8+ zombies.

Flee & Strafe

Multi-factor flee considers crowd count, health, damage taken, and proximity. Higher-ranked bots flee earlier and more decisively. During combat, bots strafe perpendicular to their target while maintaining kite distance.

Early Game Flow

Every bot follows a strict sequential startup that mirrors what a real player does in the opening rounds:

  1. Buy Weapon — Scores all nearby wallbuys using bot_score_weapon() and picks the best affordable one (prefers M14 over Olympia).
  2. Claim a Window — Finds an unoccupied barrier (200+ units from any other player/bot) and defends it for points.
  3. Buy a Door — When it accumulates 750+ points, walks to the nearest door and opens it.
  4. Full Autonomy — Early game phase ends. The decision engine takes over completely.
Elite Skip: Rank 3+ bots (Skull & Knife, Shotguns) skip the window phase at round 3+ and go straight to door buying. Elite bots don't waste time camping windows in later rounds.

How Training Works

  1. Zone Evaluation — Check for 8+ nav nodes within 500 units (open space) and 2+ exit directions.
  2. Circuit Building — Construct a ring of validated waypoints around the training center.
  3. Circuit Running — Run the waypoints, drawing zombies into a trailing horde.
  4. Danger Detection — Dot product analysis detects zombies approaching from the front. Bot reverses if blocked.
  5. Emergency Abort — Zombie breaches melee range: abort. Shotguns handles 50 units, Skull aborts at 80.
  6. Turn-and-Burn — Periodically turn and fire into the horde with full aim system. Magazine-aware: checks if clip is below 30% first and reloads before turning.
  7. Lap Counting — Auto-rebuilds circuit after set laps. 200-tick abort counter prevents stale training.

Last Zombie Save & AFK Training

At the end of every round, when 1–2 zombies remain, every bot stops shooting. No exceptions. This is hardcoded.

  1. One bot is designated the anchor and actively kites the last zombie.
  2. All other bots can still buy perks, weapons, and PaP — but cannot shoot.
  3. The anchor runs a circular pattern: sprint away if too close, circle sideways in the sweet spot, walk back if too far.
AFK Safe: The anchor pattern runs indefinitely. You can walk away from the keyboard and come back whenever you want. The zombie will still be alive.

Round-Based Scaling

Bots improve as rounds increase through a logarithmic curve: fast improvement early, diminishing returns later. Stats scale up to a 0.40 cap around round 16.

Affected stats: aim scatter, headshot chance, reaction time, burst pause, revive delay, powerup range, crowd tolerance, and training skill.

Elite Scaling: For rounds 40+, a secondary linear factor kicks in (capped at 0.15). Applied to headshot chance, crowd flee count, and powerup range only. Keeps bots viable in ultra-high rounds (R40–R70+) without breaking early game balance.

Downed Bot Behavior

Downed bots don't just lie there. They actively fight back with their last stand pistol:

  • 500-unit scan range for nearby zombies
  • Rank 3+ bots land headshots 70% of the time using j_head tag targeting (accurate for crawlers and bosses)
  • Crawl toward the nearest teammate for faster revive
  • Resource locks (box, PaP, debris) are automatically cleared on down

Announcer System

Easter Egg Support

Bots assist with every Easter Egg step on all 6 maps. Toggled via bo2_zm_bots_ee (enabled by default).

Design Philosophy: "Assist Everything, Finish Nothing." Bots help with every step but will never complete the final one. The human player always gets the honor of finishing the Easter Egg.

Health Gate Safety

If a bot drops below 30% health during an EE objective, the goal pauses for 3 seconds so combat/flee systems take over. Resumes automatically once stabilized.

Safety & Stability

Engineered for crash-free, long-session gameplay. Every system has safety guards.

Loop Safety

  • 74 endon guards across all functions for death, disconnect, and game_ended
  • 652 IsDefined checks validate bot state before every access
  • 200-tick training abort counter prevents indefinite loops

Resource Management

  • Resource locks on Box, PaP, generators, and doors with automatic cleanup on disconnect and laststand
  • 42-label goal sweep on every respawn for 100% clean state
  • Rank-scaled stuck detection: Shotguns = 2.5s, Bones = 4.5s
  • Door/debris stuck prevention: auto-cancels after 4 seconds with no movement
  • Ghost player kick loop every 10 seconds

Combat Safety

  • Triple LOS enforced everywhere: threat acquisition, aim/fire, training, last stand, mannequin shooting
  • 3-frame stale clearing via los_fail_count
  • No scatter at close range (<150 units)
  • No fallback shooting: failed LOS = stop firing entirely
  • takeweapon() preserves knife, grenades, and equipment during swaps

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to configure anything?

No. The defaults (3 bots, Shotguns rank) work perfectly out of the box.

Can I use this on any BO2 Zombies map?

Yes. All 6 maps are fully supported with dedicated logic for each map's unique mechanics.

Will bots steal my ice staff on Origins?

Never. The ice staff is permanently reserved for human players.

Do bots kill the last zombie?

No. The last zombie save is hardcoded. Every round, all bots stop shooting and one kites the survivor. You can AFK safely.

Will bots complete the Easter Egg for me?

No. "Assist Everything, Finish Nothing." They help with every step but never complete the final one.

What about the "Client/Server game mismatch" error?

Fully restart Plutonium (close and reopen the launcher). This happens when you replace the GSC file without restarting, since scripts load at startup.

Can I run more than 3 bots?

Yes. Set bo2_zm_bots_count to 1–7 in the console.

Do bots work in online matches?

This mod is designed for Custom Games (local/private). It works with friends in private lobbies on Plutonium.
