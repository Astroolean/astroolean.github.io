Overview

Custom Bots is a single-file GSC mod for Call of Duty: Black Ops 2 Zombies on the Plutonium T6 client. It spawns fully autonomous AI bots that play like real human teammates.

These bots don't follow you around like bodyguards. They make their own decisions. They roam the map independently, train zombies into hordes, open doors, buy perks and weapons, pick up buildable parts, revive downed teammates, save the last zombie at the end of every round, knife in early rounds to maximize points, and fight their way through high rounds entirely on their own.

Every bot runs an autonomous decision engine that evaluates all available actions each tick and picks the smartest one based on current context: ammo, health, points, round number, nearby threats, and personality. No two bots make the same choices.

The entire system is contained in a single file: custom_bots.gsc. No compilation. No dependencies. No external tools. Drop it in and play.

Installation

Step 1 — Locate Your Plutonium T6 Folder

Open your Plutonium launcher and find your T6 installation directory. The default location is:

%LOCALAPPDATA%\Plutonium\storage\t6\

Step 2 — Navigate to Scripts

Inside the T6 folder, go to (or create):

storage/t6/scripts/zm/

Step 3 — Drop the File

Copy custom_bots.gsc into the zm folder:

storage/t6/scripts/zm/custom_bots.gsc

Step 4 — Launch and Play

Start Plutonium and begin a Custom Games Zombies match on any map. Bots spawn automatically at round 1.

Updating? If you replace the file, you must fully restart Plutonium (close and reopen). Restarting only the match causes a "Client/Server game mismatch" error because GSC scripts load at startup.

Autonomous Decision Engine

The brain of every bot. Instead of rigid priorities, each bot evaluates all available actions every tick and picks the highest utility score. This makes bots genuinely think for themselves.

How It Works

Every 100ms, bot_evaluate_best_action() scores every possible action. The bot executes whichever scored highest. Factors include ammo level, health, points, round number, nearby threats, and personality traits.

Why This Matters

A traditional priority system always buys Jug before ammo. But a smart player with 500 rounds refills ammo first — you can't survive the walk to the perk machine empty. The decision engine evaluates each bot's unique situation every single tick.

Advanced Combat Engine

Core of the bot AI. Runs every frame: target acquisition, aiming, firing, grenades, fleeing, strafing. Every component is rank-scaled.

Processing Pipeline

  1. Knife Mode — Early-round knife mode suppresses all shooting.
  2. Validate State — Alive, not climbing, not in menu.
  3. Crowd Census — Count nearby zombies.
  4. Target Acquire — Weighted scoring + triple LOS.
  5. Target Validate — Alive, visible, not stale (3-frame clearing).
  6. Flee Decision — Health, crowd, damage, proximity, training state.
  7. Grenade Check — Dense cluster evaluation.
  8. Combat Move — Perpendicular strafe + kite distance.
  9. Aim & Fire — Triple LOS, zero-fallback shooting.

Triple Line-of-Sight Verification

Every shot passes three independent checks:

  1. botsighttracepassed(ent) — Engine bot AI sight
  2. SightTracePassed(eye, centroid) — Ray to chest
  3. SightTracePassed(eye, feet+10) — Ray to feet

All three must pass. No fallback. If LOS fails, the bot stops firing entirely.

Burst Fire & Ammo Conservation

  • Burst Size — Bones: 5–20 shots (panic). Shotguns: 3–5 (disciplined).
  • Below 40% ammo — Probabilistic shot suppression via ammo_conserve stat.
  • Below 30% — Bursts capped at 3, pauses increase 50%.
  • Range-Gated — 600u normal, 400u at 50% ammo, 250u at 25%.

Reaction & Focus

Target acquisition has rank-scaled delay (Bones: 800ms, Shotguns: 50ms) with ±50ms jitter. Focus accuracy improves aim by 15% after 1s on same target, 25% after 2s.

Grenade Intelligence

  • Crowd threshold: Bones = 7 zombies, Shotguns = 5
  • Cooldown: Bones = 20s, Shotguns = 12s
  • Ammo-gated: won't throw below 25% gun ammo
  • Range band: 300–800 units only

Early Game Flow

Every bot follows a strict sequential startup mirroring real player behavior:

  1. Buy Weapon — Scores nearby wallbuys via bot_score_weapon(), picks the best affordable one.
  2. Claim Window — Finds unoccupied barrier (200+ units from others) and defends for points.
  3. Buy Door — At 750+ points, opens the nearest door.
  4. Full Autonomy — Decision engine takes over.
Elite Skip: Rank 3+ bots skip the window phase at round 3+ and go straight to doors.

Process

  1. Zone Evaluation — 8+ nav nodes within 500u, 2+ exits.
  2. Circuit Building — Ring of validated waypoints.
  3. Circuit Running — Draw zombies into trailing horde.
  4. Danger Detection — Dot product detects frontal approach; bot reverses.
  5. Emergency Abort — Zombie breaches melee range.
  6. Turn-and-Burn — Periodically fire into horde. Magazine-aware: reloads first if clip <30%.
  7. Lap Counting — Auto-rebuilds after set laps. 200-tick abort cap.

Proactive Part Seeking

Bots actively route to known part spawn areas across the entire map using hardcoded patrol waypoints. After collecting enough parts, they navigate to the build table and craft. On Origins, bots seek unpowered generators across all 6 locations.

Last Zombie Save & AFK Training

When 1–2 zombies remain, every bot stops shooting. No exceptions. Hardcoded.

  1. One bot becomes the anchor and kites the last zombie.
  2. Others can buy perks/weapons/PaP but cannot shoot.
  3. Anchor runs: sprint away if close, circle in sweet spot, walk back if far.
AFK Safe: The anchor pattern runs indefinitely. Walk away and come back whenever. The zombie will still be alive.

Round-Based Scaling

Stats improve via logarithmic curve: fast improvement early, diminishing returns later. Cap at 0.40 around R16. Affects scatter, headshot chance, reaction time, burst pause, revive delay, crowd tolerance, and training skill.

Elite Scaling (R40+): Secondary linear factor kicks in (capped 0.15). Applied to headshot chance, crowd flee, and powerup range only. Keeps bots viable R40–R70+ without breaking early game.

Downed Bot Behavior

  • 500u scan range for nearby zombies
  • Rank 3+ land headshots 70% via j_head tag targeting
  • Crawl toward nearest teammate for faster revive
  • Resource locks (box, PaP, debris) auto-cleared on down

Announcer System

HUD splashes at rounds 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 40, 50, 60, 75, 100, and 115. Clutch revives (<25% health) get special splashes. "FULL SQUAD ONLINE" when all bots are healthy.

HUD System

Bot names display with unique colors (Blue, Gold, Green). Welcome banners, notification bars, milestone splashes, and event feeds provide live status. All elements fade and destroy cleanly on game end via dual watchers.

Anti-Overlap Safeguards

  • Welcome Banner Lock — Milestone splashes and squad alerts wait until the welcome banner finishes before displaying.
  • Milestone Queuing — If a milestone is already on screen, the next one waits 4.5 seconds to prevent stacking.
  • Feed Overflow Protection — Event feed limits to 8 active entries, then pauses 1 second before resetting to prevent text pileup.
  • Bot Name Spacing — 30px vertical gap between each bot name (fontScale 1.4) prevents any overlap.

Safety & Stability

Engineered for crash-free long sessions. Every system has safety guards.

Loop Safety

  • 74 endon guards for death/disconnect/game_ended
  • 628 IsDefined checks validate bot state
  • 200-tick training abort counter

Resource Management

  • Locks on Box, PaP, generators, doors with auto-cleanup on disconnect/laststand
  • 61-label goal sweep on every stuck reset and respawn
  • Rank-scaled stuck detection: Shotguns 2.5s, Bones 4.5s
  • Ghost player kick loop every 10 seconds

Combat Safety

  • Triple LOS everywhere: acquisition, firing, training, last stand
  • 3-frame stale clearing via los_fail_count
  • No scatter at <150 units. No fallback shooting.
  • takeweapon() preserves knife/grenades/equipment

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to configure anything?

No. Defaults (3 bots, Shotguns rank) work perfectly out of the box.

Will bots steal my ice staff?

Never. Permanently reserved for humans.

Do bots kill the last zombie?

No. Hardcoded save every round. AFK safe.

Will bots finish the Easter Egg?

No. "Assist Everything, Finish Nothing." Final step is always yours.

"Client/Server game mismatch" error?

Fully restart Plutonium. Scripts load at startup.

More than 3 bots?

Set bo2_zm_bots_count to 1–7.

Online matches?

Designed for Custom Games (local/private). Works in private lobbies on Plutonium.
